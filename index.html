Privacy Policies

Privacy Policy - AnyLink URL Video Player

AnyLink does not require user registration. Saved stream URLs are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences and are never transmitted to external servers. The app uses Google AdMob to serve non-personalized and personalized advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy - Amma Studio HD

Amma Studio HD retrieves streaming configuration from Firebase Realtime Database. It does not collect personal identity information. App diagnostics and crash reports are handled via Firebase Crashlytics to improve application stability.
